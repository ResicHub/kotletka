# Kotletka — food reviews for Krasnoyarsk

A small app for friends who like to eat out: pick a place on the map, pick or add a dish, rate it 0–10,
leave a comment and a photo, see what others said, and browse the city's top dishes.

- **Works on:** Android, iPhone, PC — anything with a browser. No app store needed.
- **Languages:** English / Russian (switch in the header or in Profile).
- **Login:** your Google account. You can change your display name.

## Install on your phone

- **Android (Chrome):** open the link → menu ⋮ → *Install app* / *Add to Home screen*.
- **iPhone (Safari):** open the link → Share → *Add to Home Screen*.
- **PC (Chrome/Edge):** click the install icon in the address bar.

If you opened the link inside Telegram or another app, open it in Chrome or Safari instead:
Google blocks sign-in inside in-app browsers.

## What you can do

- **Rate dishes:** 0–10, with an optional comment and photo. One review per dish; writing again edits it.
  You can edit or delete your own reviews.
- **Add places and dishes** that are missing. Duplicates (the same place nearby, the same dish in one place) are refused.
- **Top dishes:** the best-rated dishes in the city. A dish with one 10/10 doesn't beat a dish with ten 9/10s.
- **Filters:** dish category and subcategory, "Open now", "Business lunch".
- **Business lunch:** every place has a block with yes / no / unknown, hours and a menu (text or a photo). Anyone can update it.
- **Opening hours:** anyone can set them; places show "Open now" or "Closed".
- **Want to try:** tap 🔖 on a dish or on a friend's review to save it to your list (in Profile).
  Tap **Rate** there when you've tried it.
- **Reactions:** ❤️ (useful) or 🤡 on other people's reviews. Tap again to remove.
- **Profiles:** tap a name or avatar to see that person's stats, badges and reviews.
  Tap your own avatar to upload a photo.
- **Badges:** 20 hidden badges for what you do in the app. You see one only after you earn it, with a little celebration.

## Dish categories

Main (soups, salads, meat, fish, pelmeni, pasta, other) · Fast food (pizza, burgers, sushi, shaurma, other) ·
Breakfast · Business lunch · Bakery · Desserts · Snacks · Drinks (coffee, tea, non-alcoholic, alcohol)

## Good to know

- Saving takes 1–2 seconds. Reading is cached, so the app opens instantly, even offline.
  Tap the refresh button to load the newest reviews.
- Photos are shrunk before upload and location data is removed from them.
  Anyone who has a photo's link can view it.
- Something wrong (a typo, a duplicate place, a rude review)? Tell the moderator: they can fix or remove anything.
